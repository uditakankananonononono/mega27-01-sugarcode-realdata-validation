\section{Adding evolutionary conservation to the splice-region model}
The best splice-region model so far is a logistic regression on PWM and MaxEntScan features. Neither feature sees conservation. We fetched phyloP100way at each variant position from the UCSC Genome Browser REST API for a stratified subsample of 1,501 of the 9,235 modelled variants (379 pathogenic, 1,054 genes, 0 missing). The subsample keeps the class-by-site proportions of the full set; the API rate limit (one request per second) is why we did not fetch all 9,235.
Median phyloP is 3.214 at pathogenic and 0.191 at benign positions. On its own, phyloP reaches AUROC 0.8377 (0.9108 at donors, 0.6966 at acceptors). Added to logit+MaxEnt under gene-grouped 5-fold cross-validation repeated over 20 seeds, AUROC rises from 0.963 to 0.9678. A paired bootstrap on the averaged out-of-fold predictions gives a 95\% interval for the gain of [+0.0008, +0.0091], which excludes zero.

The gain is concentrated at donor-region positions ($+3$ to $+6$), where the intronic consensus is short and conservation carries information the PWM misses. At acceptors (the polypyrimidine tract, $-3$ to $-14$), MaxEntScan already scores well and conservation adds little.
\paragraph{Caveat.} ClinVar submitters may cite conservation as supporting evidence (ACMG criteria PP3/BP4). Some of the gain may therefore be label circularity rather than new biology. We report the gain as modest and conditional on that caveat.
\paragraph{(F47) Model with conservation.} $\mathrm{logit}\,P(\mathrm{path}\mid x)=\beta_0+\boldsymbol\beta^\top\mathbf z(x)+\gamma\,\phi(x)+\delta\,\phi(x)\,\mathbb 1[\mathrm{donor}]$, with $\mathbf z$ the standardised PWM/MaxEnt/position features and $\phi$ the phyloP score; the interaction lets conservation matter differently at donors.
\paragraph{(F48) Paired bootstrap.} For resamples $b=1..B$ of variant indices $I_b$, $\Delta_b=\mathrm{AUC}(y_{I_b},\hat p^{+}_{I_b})-\mathrm{AUC}(y_{I_b},\hat p^{0}_{I_b})$; the interval is the 2.5th and 97.5th percentile of $\{\Delta_b\}$. Pairing on the same resample removes the shared sampling noise of the two models.
